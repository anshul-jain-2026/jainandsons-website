Services

Name change and name correction we help with

Whether it is a new name after marriage, a spelling mistake or a personal change, we help you prepare the papers each authority asks for.

  • Gazette notification

    Documentation and application support for publishing your new name in the Gazette through the official process, used as proof of name change by many offices.

  • Name change affidavit

    Affidavit typed on the correct stamp paper or e-stamp, with notary assistance, declaring your old and new name.

  • Aadhaar name change

    Help preparing supporting documents and guidance on the UIDAI update process for name change or correction.

  • Marksheet and certificate name change

    Affidavit, Gazette and application papers for school, board or university marksheets and certificates. The change is made by the board or university.

  • All other documents

    PAN card, passport, voter ID, driving licence, bank accounts, property papers and other records where your name needs to match.

Please note: We prepare documents and guide you. Final updates are made by the concerned authority, and each has its own rules, fees and timelines.

Frequently asked questions

Name change in Agra: FAQs

What documents are needed for a name change in Agra?

Usually your ID proof, a document showing your current name, passport-size photographs and the new name you want. Marriage certificate or other supporting papers may be needed depending on the reason.

Is a Gazette notification required for a name change?

Not for every document. Some authorities, boards and offices ask for a Gazette notification, while others accept an affidavit. Tell us which document you want to change and we will explain what is usually asked.

How do I change my name on Aadhaar?

Aadhaar name updates follow the process and document list set by UIDAI, and the number of times a name can be updated may be limited. We help you prepare the supporting documents and guide you on the steps.

Can I change my name on a marksheet or certificate?

Boards and universities have their own procedures, forms and fees. We help with the affidavit, Gazette and application documents, and the change itself is made by the concerned board or university.

How long does a name change take?

It depends on the document and the authority involved. Affidavits can be prepared quickly, while Gazette publication and board or office updates take longer. Contact us to know the usual steps for your case.

Can I start a name change without visiting the shop?

Yes, for eligible services. You can share details and document photos on WhatsApp and get guidance and a draft. Signatures, notary and some official steps may need a visit.
